What you will do

On each page you will hear six versions of the same excerpt, labelled A to F. Listen to all six, compare them freely, then rate each one on two separate scales.

Scale 1 — Audio Quality

Rate the audio quality of this excerpt from 0 to 100, judging only degradations in the sound itself — added noise, distortion, coloration, or unnatural sounds that seem not to belong in the recording. Do not judge how wide or spatial it sounds.
100 = completely clean and natural, no audible artifacts; 50 = audible artifacts but acceptable; 0 = severe artifacts/distortion.

Scale 2 — Spatial Imaging

Rate the spatial imaging of this excerpt from 0 to 100, judging only the stereo image — sense of width, the placement of sources, and the stability of that image (sources stay in-place i.e., do not move or have an unnatural spread). Do not judge audio quality, noise, or distortion.
100 = wide, natural, fixed stereo image; 50 = narrow or somewhat unstable; 0 = no stereo image (mono/collapsed), unbalanced, badly unstable.

The two scales are independent. A version can sound clean but flat, or spacious but distorted. Use the full range of each scale.
